Colour = 10 log₁₀(observed matched energy / mean quiet-background matched energy). The denominator is estimated separately for every template and I/Q orientation. This includes residual clutter; it is not a calibrated thermal SNR. A fixed noise-only filter averages 0 dB in linear units; searching a bank raises the largest noise peak.
